**Duration:** 2024
**Advisor:** Dr. Rui Ning, Old Dominion University

Evaluated the deployment feasibility of BitNet, a compact large language model, across heterogeneous hardware platforms. Benchmarked both inference speed and predictive accuracy on resource-constrained and high-performance devices, revealing a nontrivial correlation: model inference remained fast universally, but accuracy improved commensurately with hardware capacity.

**Keywords:** Model Compression, Edge AI Benchmarking, Neural Network Quantization
